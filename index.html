Spelling

Boards that grow with your child

Start with 4 big letters. Move to 9. Then the full 26-letter letterboard, and a QWERTY keyboard when they are ready. Every letter speaks the moment it is tapped, and a typed word is read out loud.

This is live, tap the letters. In the app, every letter can wear its own color.

The real thing: WATER, typed and spoken in the app.

Talk

Tap pictures, build real sentences

The Talk board thinks ahead. Tap "I want", and it offers food, drink, comfort. Tap "I feel", and it offers feelings, and where it hurts. Each choice suggests the next natural words, then the whole sentence is spoken with one tap. Parents can add their own buttons with photos from the iPad.

"I feel, pain, in my tooth." From a child who could not say where it hurt.

Tap tiles to build a sentence…
Voices

More than 40 real voices

Calm

Calm music and binaural beats

Three gentle, evolving soundscapes play softly under the whole app. Press a track to hear 10 seconds, then add a binaural beat on top: 10 Hz to calm, 40 Hz to focus. Mix them, or turn the beat off. In the app, parents set it all in Settings.

🎧 Binaural beat

Headphones needed. The beat lives in the difference between your ears, and layers over whatever track is playing.
